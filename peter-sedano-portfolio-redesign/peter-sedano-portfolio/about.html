A LITTLE ABOUT ME

My story

I enjoy the process of taking an idea, figuring out how it might work, and gradually turning it into a project. The part I find exciting is learning through the building process: trying things, solving problems, fixing mistakes, and seeing progress.

My interests sit between software and hardware. I like exploring web applications and digital tools, while also being curious about microcontrollers, sensors, and systems that interact with the real world.

I'm still developing my skills, and this portfolio is a place to document what I'm learning, what I've built, and what I want to explore next.
